Why I built it

I noticed many Christian wallpaper apps paired verses with generic backgrounds and lacked automation, personalization, and a more meaningful user experience. Catholic Wallpaper was built to connect Scripture with devotional imagery while giving users tools to set, save, share, and hear the daily verse.
